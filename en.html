Ruoqu shows which store is cheapest for your basket.

Ruoqu is a Finnish grocery price comparison app for iPhone and Android. It compares the price of your own shopping basket in nearby K Group, S Group and Lidl stores. Prices come from receipts shared by users.

How Ruoqu works

  1. Add a receiptShare a digital receipt from the store app (K-Ruoka, S-mobiili, Lidl Plus) or photograph a paper receipt. Ruoqu reads the products, prices, store and date.
  2. Build your basketAdd the products you usually buy. Ruoqu compares basic products such as milk, bread and minced meat regardless of brand.
  3. See the cheapest storeYou see which nearby store is cheapest for your basket and how much you save.

Frequently asked questions

What is Ruoqu?

Ruoqu is a Finnish grocery price comparison app for iPhone and Android. You build your own shopping basket, and Ruoqu shows which nearby store is cheapest for it. Prices come from receipts shared by users.

How does Ruoqu know store prices?

Prices come from receipts that users add to the app. Ruoqu reads the products, prices, store and date from each receipt, so the prices are what was actually paid at the checkout.

Which stores are included?

Grocery stores of K Group, S Group and Lidl in Finland: K-Citymarket, K-Supermarket, K-Market, Prisma, S-market, Sale, Alepa and Lidl. A store has prices once a user has added a receipt from it.

How much does Ruoqu cost?

Ruoqu is free for users.

How reliable are the prices?

Each price comes from a real receipt, and the app shows when the price was seen. If there is no recent price, the app shows an estimate marked with ≈. Prices older than six months are not used.

What is stored from my receipt?

Only the products, prices, store and date. Payment card details are not stored, and the receipt image never leaves your phone. Other users only see the price of a product in a store, not who bought it.

Is the app available in English?

Yes. The app is available in Finnish, Swedish and English.

Where can I get Ruoqu?

Ruoqu is in a test phase (November 2026). On iPhone it is available by invitation through TestFlight, and on Android (version 11 or later) with an install link. Write to info@ruoqu.fi to become a tester. An App Store release is planned.
